# Calendar surface maps

Planet maps and `*-hd.jpg` files: Solar System Scope / INOVE,
https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/ — licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.
The downloaded files are unmodified. Rendering projects them onto spheres,
applies lighting, and downsamples the desktop detail maps to at most 4096px.

These maps are imagery-based composites derived from NASA data. The source notes
that unmapped areas may contain reconstructed terrain and colors are enhanced.
They are not live imagery. Visible credit appears below the calendar.

Original `earth-day.jpg`, `earth-night.jpg`, and `earth-clouds.jpg`: NASA Visible Earth.
Original `moon.jpg`: NASA SVS CGI Moon Kit / LROC. This is also the source for the
shaded lunar phase portraits in the event cards and calendar markers.

Orbital elements: JPL, https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/planets/approx_pos.html.
Sidereal rotation periods: NASA Planetary Fact Sheet,
https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/.
Planet sizes are enlarged for usability. Earth rotation uses GMST. The Moon keeps
its near side toward Earth. Other bodies use the published rotation periods;
their prime meridians and pole azimuths are illustrative, not navigation data.
